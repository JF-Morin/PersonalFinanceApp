{# Participants (shares) input, used by the manual, edit and carousel forms. Expects `share_users` (current user first), `shares_init` ({"": share}) and `split_hints` ({"": {"": share}}: the user's last split per category) — see insert_form_options in handlers/transactions.rs. - Presets cover the usual splits in one tap: two people → "Moi 100 %", "50 / 50", " 100 %"; three or more → "Moi seul", "Partagé également". - "Autre…" opens the custom split: with two people one slider (5 % steps) sets both shares, so the total is always 100 % — the thumb moves towards whoever pays more (100 % for me = thumb on my side, the left; its value is the other person's share); with more, a chip per person (the selected ones share equally). "Ajuster" shows a number input per person for exact values. - "Dernier partage": when the form's category has a last split, a suggestion applies it in one tap (nothing is applied by itself). It stays shown while the split matches it (marked "Appliqué") so the field below never jumps while the slider moves. Everything is submitted as one `shares_json` field with the enclosing form; the server checks the total again (handlers::transactions::parse_shares). #}
